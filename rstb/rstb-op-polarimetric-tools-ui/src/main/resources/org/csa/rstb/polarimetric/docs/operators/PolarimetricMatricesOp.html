Input and Output

  • For C3, C4, T3 and T4 the input is a full polarimetric SLC product with 8 bands, i.e. I and Q bands for HH, VV, HV and VH polarizations. A C3 or T3 product can also be converted to the other 3×3 form, and a C4 or T4 product down to C3 or T3.
  • For C2 the input is a dual-pol (HH+HV, VV+VH or HH+VV) or compact-pol SLC product with 4 bands. A quad-pol product is rejected; select two polarizations with Band Select first. The output bands are C11, C12_real, C12_imag and C22; for VV+VH, C11 is the VV power and C22 the VH power.
  • Since the output covariance or coherency matrix is Hermitian positive semidefinite, only 9 elements in C3 or T3 are independent, and 16 elements in C4 or T4 are independent. Therefore only the independent elements are output. For example, the following 9 bands are output: C11, C12_real, C12_imag, C13_real, C13_imag, C22, C23_real, C23_imag, C33 for covariance matrix C3.

Parameters Used

  1.    Polarimetrix Matrix: The covariance or coherency matrix type. The available types are: C2 (dual-pol or compact-pol input), C3, C4, T3 and T4.
