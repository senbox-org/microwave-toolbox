Import RADARSAT Constellation Mission

The RADARSAT Constellation Mission (RCM) is Canada's three-satellite C-band SAR constellation. SNAP reads RCM products with the RCM reader.

Mission

  • Operator: Canadian Space Agency (CSA).
  • Radar: C-band, 5.405 GHz (wavelength about 5.5 cm).
  • Orbit: three satellites in one sun-synchronous orbital plane at about 600 km, spaced 120° apart; 4-day exact revisit for the constellation.
  • Lifetime: launched 12 June 2019; operational since December 2019.
  • Modes: Spotlight (about 1 m × 3 m), high-resolution 3 m and 5 m modes, ScanSAR modes down to 100 m resolution with swaths up to 500 km, Low Noise ScanSAR, Ship Detection and a Quad-Pol mode.
  • Polarisation: single (HH, VV, HV or VH), dual, compact polarimetry (right-circular transmit, linear H and V receive) and quad-pol.

How to open

Select the zipped product (RCM*.zip) or the manifest.safe file inside the unzipped product folder (its name starts with RCM). The import dialog selects files only, not folders; the reader's file extensions are .zip and .safe.

What you get

  • SLC and GRC: i_/q_ bands per polarisation (for example i_HH) and virtual Intensity_ bands.
  • GRD, GCD: Amplitude_ bands and virtual Intensity_ bands.
  • Compact-pol channels are named RCH and RCV, for example i_RCH.
  • MLC: the compact-pol covariance matrix C2 as C11, C12_real, C12_imag and C22.
  • Tie-point grids for latitude, longitude, incidence angle and slant-range time; GCC and GCD take the map geocoding of the GeoTIFF.
  • The calibration look-up tables (σ°, β°, γ°) and noise levels, used by Calibration.

The mission and acquisition parameters SNAP uses are listed in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • Selecting product.xml alone does not open the product; select manifest.safe or the zipped product.
  • Apply Orbit File has no RCM orbit source; the reader uses the orbit in the product.

Recommended next step

Calibration, then Speckle Filter and Range-Doppler Terrain Correction. For compact-pol data, see Compact Polarimetric Stokes Parameters and Dual and compact polarimetry.
