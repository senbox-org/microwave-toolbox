Import RADARSAT-2 SAR

RADARSAT-2 is a Canadian commercial C-band SAR satellite with full polarimetric capability. SNAP reads its products with the RADARSAT-2 reader.

Mission

  • Operator: MDA, which owns and operates the satellite; developed with the Canadian Space Agency (CSA).
  • Radar: C-band, 5.405 GHz (wavelength about 5.5 cm).
  • Orbit: sun-synchronous at about 798 km, 24-day repeat cycle.
  • Lifetime: launched 14 December 2007; in operation.
  • Beam modes: Standard, Fine, Ultra-Fine, Multi-Look Fine, Spotlight, ScanSAR Narrow and Wide, and the quad-pol Standard and Fine modes; resolutions from about 1 m × 3 m to 100 m.
  • Polarisation: single (HH, HV, VV or VH), dual (HH+HV or VV+VH) and quad.

How to open

Select product.xml in the product folder, the folder itself, or the zipped product (name starting with RS2).

What you get

  • SLC: i_/q_ bands per polarisation (for example i_HH, q_HH) and virtual Intensity_ bands. Quad-pol SLC is ready for the polarimetric operators.
  • Detected products: Amplitude_ bands and virtual Intensity_ bands.
  • Tie-point grids for latitude, longitude, incidence angle and slant-range time.
  • The calibration look-up tables (lutSigma.xml, lutBeta.xml, lutGamma.xml), used by Calibration.

The mission and acquisition parameters SNAP uses are listed in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • Products delivered in NITF format are not read. Order GeoTIFF, or convert the NITF imagery with GDAL.
  • Apply Orbit File has no RADARSAT-2 orbit source; the reader uses the orbit in product.xml.

Recommended next step

Calibration, then Speckle Filter and Range-Doppler Terrain Correction. For quad-pol SLC: Calibration with complex output, then Polarimetric Matrices, Polarimetric Speckle Filter and Polarimetric Decomposition.

Product format specification

The format is defined in MDA's RADARSAT-2 Product Format Definition (RN-RP-51-2713), supplied with the data. Mission: CSA: RADARSAT-2 and eoPortal: RADARSAT-2.
