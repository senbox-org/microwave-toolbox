Import RADARSAT-1

RADARSAT-1 was a Canadian C-band SAR satellite. SNAP reads its CEOS products with the RADARSAT-1 reader.

Mission

  • Operator: Canadian Space Agency (CSA).
  • Radar: C-band, 5.3 GHz (wavelength about 5.6 cm), HH polarisation.
  • Orbit: sun-synchronous at about 798 km, 24-day repeat cycle.
  • Lifetime: launched 4 November 1995; stopped working after an anomaly on 29 March 2013.
  • Beam modes: Standard, Wide, Fine, ScanSAR Narrow, ScanSAR Wide and Extended (high and low incidence), with resolutions from about 8 m to 100 m.

Supported products

ProductContent
SLCSingle Look Complex, slant range
SGFPath Image: detected, ground range
SGXPath Image Plus: as SGF with finer pixel spacing
SSGMap Image: detected, map projected
SCN, SCWScanSAR Narrow and ScanSAR Wide, detected

How to open

Select the CEOS volume file (name starting with VDF or VOL). The leader, image and trailer files must be in the same folder. A zipped product whose name starts with R1_ or RS1_ can be opened directly.

What you get

  • SLC: i_HH and q_HH bands and a virtual Intensity_HH band.
  • Detected products: an Amplitude_HH band and a virtual Intensity_HH band.
  • Tie-point grids for latitude, longitude, incidence angle and slant-range time.

The mission and acquisition parameters SNAP uses are listed in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • SNAP has no RADARSAT-1 calibrator, so Calibration does not accept these products.
  • Apply Orbit File has no RADARSAT-1 orbit source; the orbit comes from the leader file.

Recommended next step
