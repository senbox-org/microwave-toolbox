Import Gaofen-3

The Gaofen3 reader (File > Import > SAR Sensors > GAOFEN-3/3B/3C) opens products from the three Gaofen-3 satellites, which share one product format.

Mission

  • Operator: China National Space Administration (CNSA); satellites built by the China Academy of Space Technology.
  • Instrument: C-band SAR at 5.4 GHz (wavelength about 5.6 cm), 12 imaging modes.
  • Orbit: sun-synchronous, about 755 km altitude.
  • Satellites: GF-3 01 launched 10 August 2016 (9 August UTC; file prefix GF3), GF-3 02 in November 2021 (GF3B) and GF-3 03 in April 2022 (GF3C).

Supported products

LevelContentRead by SNAP
L1ASingle-look complex, slant rangeYes
L1BDetected imageYes
L2Geometrically correctedNo
  • Modes: Spotlight, stripmap (ultra-fine, fine, wide fine, standard), ScanSAR (narrow, wide, global), quad-pol stripmap (two swath widths), wave and extended incidence angle.
  • Polarisations: single (HH or VV), dual (HH+HV or VV+VH) or quad (HH+HV+VH+VV).

How to open

Select the metadata file ending in .meta.xml (name starting with GF3_, GF3B_ or GF3C_), or the product folder, or a ZIP archive with one of these prefixes. The GeoTIFF images and the .rpc file must be in the same folder.

What you get

  • L1A: complex bands i_HH and q_HH (units real and imaginary) per polarisation, plus a virtual Intensity_HH.
  • L1B: Amplitude_HH per polarisation, plus a virtual Intensity_HH.
  • Geocoding from the RPC (rational polynomial coefficients) file, an incidence angle tie-point grid, and orbit state vectors that the reader reconstructs from the RPC model.
  • Acquisition parameters are in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • Radiometric Calibration does not support Gaofen-3.
  • The product type is taken from _L1A_ or _L1B_ in the file name; Level-2 products are not recognised.
  • The orbit is derived from the RPC model, not from precise orbit data, so geometric accuracy depends on the RPC.

Recommended next step

  • L1A: Multilook → Speckle Filter → Range Doppler Terrain Correction.
  • L1B: Speckle Filter → Range Doppler Terrain Correction.
  • Quad-pol L1A: Polarimetric Matrices.
