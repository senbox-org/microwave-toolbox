Import Capella

The Capella reader (File > Import > SAR Sensors > CAPELLA) opens Capella Space SAR products delivered as GeoTIFF with JSON metadata.

Mission

  • Operator: Capella Space (USA), a commercial constellation of small X-band SAR satellites.
  • Instrument: X-band SAR, 9.4–9.9 GHz (wavelength about 3 cm), up to 700 MHz bandwidth.
  • Orbit: low Earth orbits at several inclinations (about 45°, 53° and 97° sun-synchronous), roughly 450–650 km altitude depending on the launch.
  • Launch: first satellite 3 December 2018; more satellites have been launched since.

Acquisition modes: Spotlight, Sliding Spotlight and Stripmap. Polarisation: single, HH or VV.

How to open

Select the metadata file CAPELLA_..._extended.json. You can also select the product folder, the GeoTIFF when its _extended.json file lies beside it, or a ZIP archive whose name starts with CAPELLA_.

What you get

  • SLC: complex bands i_HH and q_HH (float, units real and imaginary), a virtual Intensity_HH, and tie-point grids for incidence angle and slant range time.
  • GEO and GEC: one detected band. When the metadata states the radiometric scale, the band is named Sigma0_HH, Beta0_HH or Gamma0_HH (intensity); otherwise it is Amplitude_HH with a virtual Intensity_HH. The map geocoding comes from the GeoTIFF. SNAP labels all detected products with product type GEO.
  • Orbit state vectors and acquisition parameters are in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • SICD and SIDD (NITF) and CPHD products cannot be opened. Order the GeoTIFF products.
  • GEO and GEC products are already map projected. Do not apply Range Doppler Terrain Correction to them.

Recommended next step

  • SLC: Radiometric Calibration → Multilook → Range Doppler Terrain Correction, or Create Stack for interferometry.
  • GEO and GEC: Speckle Filter or direct analysis. See Radiometry concepts.
