Import RISAT-1 / EOS-04

RISAT-1 and its follow-on EOS-04 (also called RISAT-1A) are Indian C-band SAR satellites. Both deliver a BAND_META.txt metadata file with the imagery. SNAP has two readers: RISAT-1 for RISAT-1 products and EOS-04 for EOS-04 GeoTIFF/COG products.

Both satellites image in the same modes: FRS-1 (Fine Resolution Stripmap, about 3 m, 25 km swath), FRS-2 (Fine Resolution Stripmap, quad-pol, 25 km swath), MRS (Medium Resolution ScanSAR, about 115–160 km swath), CRS (Coarse Resolution ScanSAR, about 223 km swath) and HRS (High Resolution Spotlight, about 1–2 m, 10 km). Polarisation is single, dual, circular (hybrid: circular transmit, linear receive) or full.

Supported products

  • Slant range (complex) products: read as SLC.
  • Ground range and geo-referenced (terrain corrected) products: read as detected.
  • RISAT-1: imagery in GeoTIFF or in CEOS format, one scene_ folder per polarisation.
  • EOS-04: imagery in GeoTIFF/COG only.

How to open

RISAT-1

Select BAND_META.txt, the product folder that contains it, or a .zip archive containing it. The reader accepts the product when the SatID in BAND_META.txt names RISAT, or when the field is missing (older products).

EOS-04 GeoTIFF/COG products

Select BAND_META.txt or the product folder. The SatID must name EOS-04, and the imagery must be GeoTIFF files with imagery in their names, inside the scene_ sub-folders. Unzip the delivery first (see Known limitations).

What you get

  • Slant-range products: i_ and q_ bands per polarisation (for example i_HH) and virtual Intensity_ bands.
  • Detected products: Amplitude_ bands and virtual Intensity_ bands.
  • Circular-transmit (hybrid) channels are named RCH and RCV.
  • RISAT-1: incidence-angle and slant-range-time tie-point grids. EOS-04: the geocoding of the GeoTIFF imagery.

The mission and acquisition parameters SNAP uses are listed in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • EOS-04 products in CEOS format are not read: the RISAT-1 reader rejects them by their SatID, and the EOS-04 reader needs GeoTIFF imagery.
  • Both readers claim any .zip that contains a BAND_META.txt, so a zipped EOS-04 product may be opened by the RISAT-1 reader. Unzip EOS-04 products and select BAND_META.txt.
  • Apply Orbit File has no RISAT-1 or EOS-04 orbit source; the orbit comes from the product.

Recommended next step

Calibration (supported for RISAT-1 and EOS-04), then Speckle Filter and Range-Doppler Terrain Correction. For hybrid-polarity data see Compact Polarimetric Stokes Parameters.
