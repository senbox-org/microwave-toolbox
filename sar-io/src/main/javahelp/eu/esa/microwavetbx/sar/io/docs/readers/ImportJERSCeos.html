Import JERS SAR 

JERS-1 was a Japanese Earth-resources satellite with an L-band SAR. SNAP reads its SAR products in CEOS format with the JERS CEOS reader.

Mission

  • Developer: NASDA, the National Space Development Agency of Japan (now part of JAXA).
  • Radar: L-band, 1.275 GHz (wavelength about 23.5 cm), HH polarisation.
  • Orbit: about 568 km altitude, 44-day repeat cycle.
  • Lifetime: launched 11 February 1992; operations ended in October 1998.

How to open

Select the CEOS volume file (name starting with VDF, VOL or 01, or a J1*.01 file). The leader, image and trailer files must be in the same folder. A zipped product whose name starts with J1 can be opened directly.

What you get

  • SLC: bands i and q (real and imaginary parts) and a virtual Intensity band.
  • PRI and GEC: an Amplitude band and a virtual Intensity band.
  • When a product holds more than one image file, the bands get a number suffix (Amplitude_1, Amplitude_2, ...).
  • Geocoding from the scene corners in the leader file, plus tie-point grids.

The mission and acquisition parameters SNAP uses are listed in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • SNAP has no radiometric calibrator for JERS-1, so the Calibration operator does not accept these products.
  • The Apply Orbit File operator has no orbit source for JERS-1; the orbit comes from the leader file.
  • Band names carry no polarisation; the polarisation is always HH.

Recommended next step

Multilook or Speckle Filter the detected product, then Range-Doppler Terrain Correction to map-project it.
