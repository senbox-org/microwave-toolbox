Import BIOMASS

The BIOMASS reader (File > Import > SAR Sensors > BIOMASS) opens ESA BIOMASS Level-1 SAR products and Level-2 forest products.

Mission

  • Operator: European Space Agency (ESA), Earth Explorer mission.
  • Instrument: the first spaceborne P-band SAR: 435 MHz centre frequency, 6 MHz bandwidth, wavelength about 70 cm, fully polarimetric (HH, HV, VH, VV).
  • Orbit: sun-synchronous dawn-dusk, about 666 km altitude, 3-day repeat. A tomographic phase (stacks of 7 acquisitions) is followed by an interferometric phase (stacks of 3).
  • Launch: 29 April 2025; five-year nominal mission.

How to open

Select the main XML header in the product folder (file name starting with BIO_S for Level 1 or BIO_FP for Level 2, ending in .xml), or the product folder itself, or the product ZIP archive.

What you get

  • L1A SCS and L1C STA: Amplitude_HH and Phase_HH per polarisation, plus virtual i_HH, q_HH and Intensity_HH bands, so SAR operators see a complex product.
  • L1B DGM: Amplitude_HH per polarisation plus a virtual Intensity_HH.
  • Level 1 geocoding: latitude/longitude tie-point grids from the annotation, plus orbit, Doppler and slant-to-ground-range metadata. The calibration, noise and RFI (radio-frequency interference) annotations are loaded into the original metadata when present.
  • Level 2: geophysical bands with map geocoding, for example Forest_Height (m), Forest_Height_Quality, Forest_Disturbance, Probability_of_Change, Computed_Forest_Mask, Ground_Notch_HH/VH/VV, AGB and AGB_Std_Dev (t/ha).
  • Mission and acquisition parameters are in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • Level-2 products hold geophysical values. Radiometric Calibration refuses them; calibrate the Level-1 product instead.
  • P-band data are affected by radio-frequency interference. Check the RFI annotation before interpreting backscatter.
  • Outputs of the BioPAL prototype processor are not opened by default. An experimental reader for them is enabled with the Java system property biomass.biopal.reader=true.

Recommended next step

  • Level 1: Apply Orbit File (BIOMASS precise orbit) → Radiometric Calibration → Range Doppler Terrain Correction.
  • Quad-pol SCS: Polarimetric Matrices; see Polarimetry concepts.
  • Level 2: the products are already geocoded; use them directly in analysis tools.

Product format specification

ESA publishes the BIOMASS L1a/b/c and Level-2 product format specifications on the BIOMASS documentation page: https://earth.esa.int/eogateway/missions/biomass/documentation. Mission pages: https://earth.esa.int/eogateway/missions/biomass, https://www.eoportal.org/satellite-missions/biomass.
