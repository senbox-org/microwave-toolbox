Import COSMO-SkyMed

SNAP reads COSMO-SkyMed first-generation (CSK) and second-generation (CSG) products with two readers: one for HDF5 files (menu File > Import > SAR Sensors > COSMO-SKYMED) and one for CSG GeoTIFF files. File > Open Product picks the right reader for either format.

Mission

  • Operator: Italian Space Agency (ASI) and Italian Ministry of Defence (dual use).
  • Instrument: X-band SAR at 9.6 GHz (wavelength about 3.1 cm).
  • Orbit: sun-synchronous dawn-dusk, 619.6 km altitude, 16-day repeat cycle per satellite.
  • CSK: four satellites launched 8 June 2007, 9 December 2007, 25 October 2008 and 6 November 2010. CSK-3 was decommissioned in April 2022 and CSK-1 in April 2025.
  • CSG: CSG-1 launched 18 December 2019, CSG-2 on 31 January 2022 and CSG-3 on 3 January 2026.

Supported products

  • Modes: Spotlight, Stripmap (CSK HIMAGE), PingPong, ScanSAR (CSK WideRegion and HugeRegion), and on CSG an experimental quad-pol mode.
  • Polarisations: single (HH, VV, HV or VH); PingPong alternates two polarisations.
  • Formats: HDF5 (.h5) for CSK and CSG; GeoTIFF with an .attribs.xml metadata file for CSG.

How to open

  • HDF5: select the .h5 file. Its name must start with CSK or CSG.
  • GeoTIFF: with File > Open Product, select the .tif image or the .attribs.xml file (name starting with CSK or CSG).
  • ZIP archives are not opened directly: unpack them first.

What you get

  • SCS: complex bands i_HH and q_HH (units real and imaginary) per polarisation, plus a virtual Intensity_HH.
  • DGM, GEC, GTC: Amplitude_HH per polarisation, plus a virtual Intensity_HH.
  • Tie-point grids (geolocation, and for HDF5 also incidence angle and slant range time) and orbit state vectors from the product. Both generations are labelled mission CSK.
  • Acquisition parameters are in the Abstracted Metadata.

Known limitations

  • Complex (SCS) ScanSAR HugeRegion products are refused by the HDF5 reader.
  • Radiometric Calibration does not support the SCS_U product type.
  • Apply Orbit File has no COSMO-SkyMed orbit type; SNAP uses the orbit in the product.

Recommended next step

  • DGM: Radiometric Calibration → Speckle Filter → Range Doppler Terrain Correction.
  • SCS for interferometry: Create Stack → Interferogram.
  • GEC and GTC are already map projected; do not terrain-correct them again.
