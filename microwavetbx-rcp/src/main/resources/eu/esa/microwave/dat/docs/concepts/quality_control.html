SAR Concepts: Quality Control and Troubleshooting

Quality Control and Troubleshooting

Most bad SAR results come from a small set of causes: a step skipped, steps run in the wrong order, or a parameter left at a default that does not suit the data. This page lists the symptoms you will see, how to confirm the cause with SNAP's own tools, and the fix.

General checks

Before you look for a processing error, find out what has already been done to the product.

Phase jumps at burst seams Enhanced Spectral Diversity not run, or its overlap estimates are noisy because coherence is low Processing_Graph: an Enhanced-Spectral-Diversity node after Back-Geocoding; zoom on the seams in Phase_ifg Back-Geocoding, then ESD, on split burst products, before TOPSAR-Deburst (TOPS).
A smooth phase ramp across the scene Annotated or restituted orbits; ionosphere; troposphere orbit_state_vector_file of each date Precise orbits (Orbits and ETAD); for long-wavelength residuals see Atmosphere.
Fringes everywhere, following the relief Topographic phase not removed. In Interferogram, Subtract flat-earth phase is on by default, Subtract topographic phase is off. Processing_Graph: Interferogram parameters, or a TopoPhaseRemoval node Tick Subtract topographic phase or run Topographic Phase Removal (InSAR).
Noise only, coherence near zero everywhere Long temporal or large perpendicular baseline; images from different tracks InSAR Stack Overview (dates, B⊥); REL_ORBIT, PASS Choose a shorter pair; use the same relative orbit, sub-swath and polarisation (Coherence).
Coherence of 0.1–0.3 over water or other surfaces that cannot be coherent Estimator bias, larger for small windows: about 0.1 for the default 10 × 10 window (more in practice, since adjacent pixels are correlated), about 0.2–0.3 for 3 × 3 to 5 × 5 Statistics of the coherence band over water Use a larger window; compare only values from the same window (Coherence).
Steps of 2π (one fringe) in unwrapped phase; values differ across water or gaps Unwrapping errors in noisy or disconnected areas Compare with reference points of known motion; form unwrapped triplet sums (Unw_ab + Unw_bc − Unw_ac) in Band Maths: a spatial step of 2π marks an error, a constant offset does not Multilook and filter before unwrapping; mask low coherence (Unwrapping).
Uplift where subsidence was expected (or the reverse) Sign convention, reference pair order, or reference point Compare with GNSS in the line of sight See Displacement.

SBAS Inversion's closure_phase_rms band wraps each triplet closure into (−π, π] before taking the RMS. It shows phase inconsistency (decorrelation, short-baseline bias) but is blind to whole-cycle unwrapping errors, which close to a multiple of 2π and wrap to zero. Where no triplet has all three pairs above the coherence threshold (for example everywhere in a sequential network) it is no-data. Check unwrapping with reference points or redundant pairs, as above (Time series).

Polarimetry

SymptomLikely causeHow to check in SNAPFix
Many pixels at the scene minimum in Freeman-Durden or Yamaguchi surface or double-bounce bands The volume model takes more power than the co-pol channels hold; SNAP clamps the negative power Histogram of the band: a spike at the minimum Larger window; orientation angle correction first; or a model-free or eigenvalue decomposition (Decompositions).
Cities and steep slopes show strong volume scattering Cross-pol power from oriented buildings and azimuth slopes is assigned to volume Compare with H-A-Alpha: high volume but low entropy is suspect Run Orientation Angle Correction before the decomposition.
van Zyl shows surface scattering on buildings parallel to the track SNAP labels the larger co-pol eigenvalue as surface; where double bounce dominates, VanZyl_surf_b and VanZyl_dbl_r are swapped Compare with Pauli_r Use Freeman-Durden or Yamaguchi for double bounce in urban areas.

Common mistakes

  • Debugging a final product instead of the intermediate ones. Open each step's output and check it.
  • Assuming a step was run because it is in your graph file. The product's Processing_Graph shows what was actually applied.
  • Reading a low closure_phase_rms as proof of correct unwrapping.
